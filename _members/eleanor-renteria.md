Eleanor Renteria is a undergraduate student under the School of Molecular Sciences Medicinal Chemistry program. Eleanor also studies under the School of Life Sciences SOLUR program through Dr. Holechek's lab where her work sits at the intersection of biosocial mechanisms, clinical data translation, and health equity research. She is currently developing the Adult Adverse Experience Index (AAEI), a ten-domain composite instrument designed to quantify cumulative adversity in adult populations using the NIH All of Us research database. Alongside this work, she is planning the Fall 2026 reseach immersion program, with a focus on how environmental and nutritional stressors disrupt intestinal integrity at the cellular level. Her long-term goal is to train as a physician scientist through an MD-PhD prgram with research focused on how human experience becomes encoded in biological systems and how those patterns shape health outcomes across populations. She approaches every project with the conviction that rigorous science and accountability to patients are not competing values.
