# Welcome to the Holechek Laboratory

We study the cellular and molecular mechanisms that shape human health across diverse populations. By integrating wet laboratory science with large-scale biobank data from the All of Us Research Program, our work moves between the bench and population health.

Our team is based at Arizona State University in the School of Life Sciences. We are committed to rigorous, reproducible science and to building a lab culture where researchers at every stage are supported to do their best work.

## Training and Inclusion

Our training philosophy draws on the SOLUR and iSTEM SOLUR Prep models developed at Arizona State University under Dr. Susan Holechek, whose work expanding undergraduate research access for online and first-generation students has been recognized with ASU's Catalyst Award for inclusion and the Outstanding Faculty Mentor Award.

We participate in the [School of Life Sciences Undergraduate Research (SOLUR)](https://sols.asu.edu/research/solur) program and its entry-level pipeline, iSTEM SOLUR Prep, which provides structured research onboarding for students with limited prior laboratory or computational experience. First-generation college students, transfer students, and online students are explicitly welcome and actively recruited into our training programs.

We also work within broader efforts to ensure that online students have equitable access to undergraduate research experiences, which remain one of the most consistently gatekept high-impact practices in higher education.
